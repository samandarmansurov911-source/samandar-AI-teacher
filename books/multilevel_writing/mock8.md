# MOCK 8 — Campus Canteen

## Topshiriqlar

**Vaziyat:** Siz universitet talabasisiz. Oshxona menyusi yangilanmoqda. Oshxona mudiri quyidagi emailni yubordi:

> Dear Student,
> We are currently reviewing our food options in the campus canteen. What meals or snacks would you like to see added or improved? We would love to hear your suggestions to make the canteen better for all students.
> Best regards,
> Canteen Manager

**Task 1.1 – Informal Letter.** Write to your classmate about the canteen changes and what you think should be added. *Write about 50 words.*

**Task 1.2 – Formal Letter.** Write to the canteen manager suggesting improvements to the menu. *Write 120–150 words.*

**Task 2 – Forum Post.** Online discussion: *"Should people focus more on their career or on work-life balance?"* Give your opinion and support it with examples. *Write 180–200 words.*

### Task 1.2 – Formal Letter

Dear Canteen Manager,

Thank you for asking for student feedback regarding the menu update. I appreciate your effort to improve the dining experience on campus.

As a regular customer, I would like to suggest a few additions. First, it would be great to have more healthy meal options, such as grilled chicken wraps, vegetable stir-fry, and fresh salads. Currently, most choices are quite heavy and fried, which doesn't suit everyone's diet.

In addition, including a wider variety of vegetarian, vegan, and halal dishes would make the canteen more inclusive for students with dietary restrictions. Offering affordable meal combos, including a drink and snack, could also be popular.

Finally, I recommend adding a feedback box or digital form so students can continue to share their ideas regularly.

Thank you again for considering our input. I look forward to seeing the updated menu soon.

Best regards,
Ayan Malik

### Task 2 – Forum Post

I believe people should prioritize work-life balance over focusing solely on their career. While professional success is important, overworking can lead to burnout, stress, and even a decline in overall well-being. For instance, research has shown that employees who maintain a healthy work-life balance are often more productive, creative, and engaged at work. Companies like Google and Microsoft have recognized this and implemented flexible work policies to support their employees' personal lives, ultimately benefiting both the workers and the organization.

Focusing too much on one's career at the expense of personal time can also strain relationships and diminish personal happiness. A workaholic mindset may result in missing out on important life moments, such as spending time with family or pursuing hobbies that bring joy. For example, a person who spends long hours at work might miss their child's school play or a friend's wedding, which can affect their emotional health and relationships.

In the long run, a balanced approach—where both career goals and personal time are valued—leads to a more fulfilling and sustainable life. It's not about choosing one over the other, but finding a harmony that supports both professional growth and personal happiness.
