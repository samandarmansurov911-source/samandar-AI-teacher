# MOCK 6 — Hotel Stay

## Topshiriqlar

**Vaziyat:** Siz ta'til paytida mehmonxonada to'xtadingiz. Ketganingizdan so'ng mehmonxona ma'muriyatidan xabar keldi:

> Dear Guest,
> We hope you enjoyed your recent stay with us. We strive to offer the best service. Could you please share your thoughts about your experience? What did you like most? How can we improve?
> Sincerely,
> Hotel Management

**Task 1.1 – Informal Letter.** Write a letter to your friend about your stay at the hotel. Share what you liked and disliked. *Write about 50 words.*

**Task 1.2 – Formal Letter.** Write a letter to the hotel management. Provide your feedback and suggest ways to improve their service. *Write 120–150 words.*

**Task 2 – Forum Post.** Online discussion: *"Do advertisements influence people too much?"* Give your opinion and explain with examples. *Write 180–200 words.*

## Javoblar

### Task 1.1 – Informal Letter

Hey Lily,

I just got back from my trip and stayed at a really nice hotel! The room was clean and the staff were super friendly. I especially loved the rooftop pool. But the breakfast could've been better—it lacked variety. Still, I'd stay there again. Let's plan a trip together soon!

Cheers,
Nina

### Task 1.2 – Formal Letter

Dear Hotel Management,

Thank you for your message. I recently stayed at your hotel during my holiday and would like to share my feedback.

Overall, I had a pleasant experience. The room was clean, well-maintained, and very comfortable. I especially appreciated the friendly and helpful staff, who made me feel welcome throughout my stay. The rooftop pool was a highlight—it offered great views and a relaxing atmosphere.

However, I believe there is room for improvement in the breakfast service. While the quality of food was decent, the options were limited, especially for vegetarian guests. Including more fresh fruits, healthier choices, and some international dishes would greatly enhance the experience.

I would also recommend adding more signage around the hotel to help guests find amenities more easily.

Thank you once again for your hospitality. I look forward to staying with you again in the future.

Sincerely,
Nina Carter

### Task 2 – Forum Post

I believe advertisements do influence people significantly, often more than they realize. In today's world, ads are everywhere—on TV, social media, billboards, and even in our apps. They're designed not just to inform but to persuade, often shaping consumer behavior and preferences. For example, targeted ads on platforms like Instagram or YouTube use data to tailor content to individual tastes, making products seem more desirable by showcasing them at the right moment.

One notable example is the success of fast food chains using advertisements that appeal to emotional triggers, like comfort or convenience. Studies have shown that children, in particular, are highly susceptible to these messages, often making food choices based on ads they've seen rather than nutritional value.

While ads can be informative, they also create unrealistic expectations. Beauty product ads, for instance, often promote an idealized version of beauty that many people feel pressured to achieve, contributing to low self-esteem and body image issues.

Overall, while advertisements can inform and offer convenience, they undeniably influence decisions in subtle ways that sometimes have negative consequences. It's important for consumers to be more aware of these influences and make more conscious choices.
