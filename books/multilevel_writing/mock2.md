# MOCK 2 — Cooking Course

## Topshiriqlar

**Vaziyat:** Siz yaqinda mahalladagi jamoat markazida pazandalik kursida qatnashdingiz. Bir necha kundan so'ng kurs tashkilotchisidan email keldi:

> Dear Participant,
> Thank you for attending the cooking course last week. We would love to hear your feedback. What did you enjoy the most? What improvements can we make to future courses?
> Best regards,
> The Organizer

**Task 1.1 – Informal Letter.** Write a letter to your friend, who also likes cooking. Share your experience and thoughts about the course. *Write about 50 words.*

**Task 1.2 – Formal Letter.** Write a letter to the course organizer. Express your opinions, say what you liked, and suggest improvements. *Write 120–150 words.*

**Task 2 – Forum Post.** Online discussion: *"Should people work from home or in the office?"* Give your opinion and support it with examples. *Write 180–200 words.*

## Javoblar

### Task 1.1 – Informal Letter

Hey Lisa,

I just finished a cooking course at the local community center last week—it was so much fun! We learned how to make Italian and Thai dishes, and the instructor was super helpful. I only wish the class sizes were smaller. You'd have loved it! Let's join the next one together.

Talk soon,
Emma

### Task 1.2 – Formal Letter

Dear Organizer,

Thank you for organizing the recent cooking course. I truly enjoyed the experience and wanted to share my feedback.

The best part of the course was the hands-on practice. Learning to prepare Italian pasta and Thai curry from scratch was both fun and educational. The instructor was knowledgeable and approachable, and I appreciated how clearly the steps were explained.

However, I think a few improvements could make future courses even better. Firstly, the class size was a bit too large, which made it difficult at times to get individual attention. Reducing the number of participants or increasing the number of instructors would help. Secondly, printed or digital recipe handouts would be useful so we can try the dishes at home.

Overall, it was a well-run and enjoyable course, and I look forward to attending more in the future.

Best regards,
Emma Thompson

### Task 2 – Forum Post

In my opinion, working from home is a better option for most people, especially in today's digital world. It offers more flexibility, improves work-life balance, and can even increase productivity.

Firstly, working from home allows people to manage their time more effectively. For example, without the need to commute, employees can use that extra time to rest or complete tasks, leading to better focus and energy during working hours. A friend of mine who works in IT said he gets more done at home because there are fewer distractions compared to the office.

Secondly, remote work improves work-life balance. People can spend more time with their families or take care of personal matters more easily. This leads to greater job satisfaction and mental well-being.

However, working in an office has its advantages too, such as easier communication and stronger team bonding. In-person meetings can help solve problems faster and improve collaboration.

Still, with today's technology, virtual meetings and team chats make remote communication effective.

In conclusion, while both options have benefits, I believe working from home is more suitable for the modern workplace, especially when combined with occasional office visits. It provides freedom, comfort, and higher productivity.
