# MOCK 5 — New Office

## Topshiriqlar

**Vaziyat:** Siz yangi ofis binosiga ko'chayotgan kompaniyada ishlaysiz. Kadrlar (HR) bo'limidan quyidagi xabar keldi:

> Dear Employee,
> As you know, our company will move to a new office next month. We are gathering suggestions for the new space. What kind of features or facilities would help you work better? Your ideas are welcome.
> Best,
> HR Team

**Task 1.1 – Informal Letter.** Write a message to your coworker. Share your feelings and ideas about the new office. *Write about 50 words.*

**Task 1.2 – Formal Letter.** Write to the HR department. Express your opinions and make useful suggestions for the new workplace. *Write 120–150 words.*

**Task 2 – Forum Post.** Online discussion: *"Is online learning as effective as traditional classroom learning?"* Give your opinion and support it with examples. *Write 180–200 words.*

## Javoblar

Did you see the HR message about the new office? I'm excited about the move! I really hope the new place has more natural light and a quiet area for focused work. A small lounge or coffee corner would be great too. What would you suggest? Let's send in our ideas!

Cheers,
Mark

### Task 1.2 – Formal Letter

Dear HR Team,

Thank you for giving employees the opportunity to share suggestions regarding the new office space. I'm excited about the move and appreciate your efforts to create a better work environment.

One important feature I would recommend is improved natural lighting. It helps boost focus and creates a more positive atmosphere. A quiet zone or designated focus area would also benefit employees who need to concentrate without distractions.

Additionally, having small breakout areas or informal meeting spots could encourage better collaboration among teams. A comfortable lounge with a coffee machine or snack station would also support short breaks and help recharge energy during busy days.

Lastly, it would be useful to have sufficient storage space and ergonomic furniture to ensure comfort and organization.

I hope these ideas help as you design the new space. Thank you again for including us in this important transition.

Best regards,
Mark Thompson

### Task 2 – Forum Post

In my opinion, online learning can be as effective as traditional classroom learning, but it depends on several factors like the learning environment, the subject matter, and the learner's self-discipline.

Online learning offers flexibility, allowing students to access materials at their own pace and from anywhere in the world. For instance, platforms like Coursera or Khan Academy provide courses from top universities, making education more accessible and personalized to each learner's needs.

However, traditional classroom learning offers direct interaction with instructors and peers, fostering social learning and providing immediate feedback. For example, in subjects like mathematics or science, the ability to ask questions in real-time and collaborate with classmates can significantly enhance understanding.

The key difference is that online learning requires greater self-motivation and time management skills, which may be challenging for some students. Traditional learning environments, on the other hand, provide a structured setting that can keep students engaged and focused.

In conclusion, both forms of learning have their strengths, and when combined, they can offer a well-rounded and effective educational experience.
