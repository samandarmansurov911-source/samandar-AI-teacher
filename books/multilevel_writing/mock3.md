# MOCK 3 — University Dormitory

## Topshiriqlar

**Vaziyat:** Siz umumiy yotoqxonada yashaydigan universitet talabasisiz. Yaqinda yotoqxona mudiri quyidagi xabarni yubordi:

> Dear Resident,
> We are planning to make some changes in the dormitory to improve students' comfort. What changes would you like to see? Also, we are considering organizing weekend activities. What kinds of activities do you suggest?
> Regards,
> Dormitory Manager

**Task 1.1 – Informal Letter.** Write a letter to your roommate, sharing your thoughts on possible changes and weekend events. *Write about 50 words.*

**Task 1.2 – Formal Letter.** Write a letter to the dormitory manager. Give your ideas and express your thoughts politely. *Write 120–150 words.*

**Task 2 – Forum Post.** Online discussion: *"Do social media platforms bring people closer together or push them apart?"* Give your opinion and support it with examples. *Write 180–200 words.*

## Javoblar

### Task 1.1 – Informal Letter

Hey Alex,

Did you see the manager's message? They're planning some changes in the dorm. I think better Wi-Fi and soundproof study rooms would be great! Also, they want to organize weekend events—maybe movie nights or cooking competitions? What do you think? Let's suggest some ideas together.

Talk soon,
Mira

### Task 1.2 – Formal Letter

Dear Dormitory Manager,

Thank you for reaching out and asking for our input. I appreciate your efforts to improve our living conditions.

One change I would suggest is upgrading the internet connection, as many students rely on it for online classes and research. Also, it would be helpful to install better lighting in the study rooms and provide more comfortable chairs in the common areas.

Regarding weekend activities, organizing movie nights, game tournaments, or cultural food events would be exciting. These types of activities can help students relax and build friendships. Workshops on stress management or job interview skills might also be useful.

Overall, I believe these improvements and events will greatly enhance the student experience in the dormitory. Thank you again for considering our opinions.

Kind regards,
Mira Patel

### Task 2 – Forum Post

In my opinion, social media platforms bring people closer together by making communication easier and helping maintain relationships over long distances.

Firstly, social media allows people to stay connected no matter where they are. For example, I have a cousin who lives abroad, and we keep in touch regularly through Instagram and video calls on WhatsApp. Without social media, we would probably lose contact. It also helps people reconnect with old friends and classmates, which wouldn't be possible otherwise.

Secondly, social media provides a platform for people to share their lives, opinions, and experiences. This can create a sense of community and support, especially for those who may feel isolated. During the COVID-19 pandemic, for instance, many people turned to social media to stay in touch, join online events, and share important information.

However, it's true that social media can sometimes lead to misunderstandings or feelings of jealousy. People may compare themselves to others or argue online. Still, I believe these issues depend more on how individuals use the platforms.

In conclusion, while social media has some downsides, it mainly brings people closer by helping them connect, communicate, and share their lives more easily.
