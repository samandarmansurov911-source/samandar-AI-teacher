# MOCK 9 — Bus Routes

## Topshiriqlar

**Vaziyat:** Siz har kuni mahalliy avtobus xizmatidan foydalanasiz. Transport boshqarmasi avtobus yo'nalishlarini yangilamoqda va fikr so'ramoqda:

> Dear Passenger,
> We are reviewing our current bus routes to serve you better. If you have any suggestions or concerns about the timing, frequency, or coverage of our routes, please let us know.
> Thank you,
> Public Transport Department

**Task 1.1 – Informal Letter.** Write to your friend who also uses the bus about the upcoming changes. *Write about 50 words.*

**Task 1.2 – Formal Letter.** Write to the Public Transport Department giving your thoughts and suggestions. *Write 120–150 words.*

**Task 2 – Forum Post.** Online discussion: *"Is it better to have many friends or a few close friends?"* Give your opinion and explain with examples. *Write 180–200 words.*

## Javoblar

### Task 1.1 – Informal Letter

Hey Sam,

Did you see that the bus service is updating its routes? I think it's a great chance to ask for more buses during rush hour and maybe a new route that goes closer to our campus. The current timing is sometimes too tight for morning classes. Let's send our ideas!

Cheers,
Ali

### Task 1.2 – Formal Letter

Dear Public Transport Department,

Thank you for inviting feedback on the current bus routes. I use your service daily and appreciate the efforts to improve public transport in our area.

One issue I've noticed is the overcrowding on buses during morning and evening rush hours. Increasing the frequency of buses, especially between 7:00–9:00 AM and 5:00–7:00 PM, would greatly improve the experience for daily commuters.

Also, many students and office workers travel to the university area, but the nearest stop requires a long walk. A new route or an extension that goes closer to the campus would be helpful.

Lastly, I suggest better synchronization between different route timings to reduce waiting times during transfers. Displaying real-time bus tracking on a mobile app or digital screens at major stops could also enhance service reliability.

Thank you again for considering public input. I look forward to seeing the improvements soon.

Sincerely,
Ali Raza

### Task 2 – Forum Post

I believe it's better to have a few close friends rather than many acquaintances. While having a large circle of friends can be fun and offer variety, it's the deep, meaningful connections that truly enrich our lives. Close friends are the ones who truly understand you, support you during tough times, and share life's most significant moments with you. For example, when going through a personal challenge, it's often the close friends who are there to offer genuine support and advice, not just surface-level interaction.

Having a few close friends also allows for stronger, more authentic relationships. It's difficult to maintain deep connections with too many people, as true friendship requires time and emotional investment. Studies have shown that quality friendships are linked to better mental health and overall happiness. Someone with just a few trusted friends is more likely to feel supported, valued, and secure in their social circle.

While having many friends can provide a wide range of social experiences, the depth and trust in a few close friendships are far more rewarding in the long run. It's these relationships that provide stability and fulfillment, far beyond the fleeting nature of many casual friendships.
