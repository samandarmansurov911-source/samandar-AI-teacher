# MOCK 7 — Local Park

## Topshiriqlar

**Vaziyat:** Siz mahalladagi bog'ga muntazam borasiz. Shahar kengashi fikr-mulohaza so'rab xabar yubordi:

> Dear Resident,
> We are planning to renovate the local park. We would appreciate your suggestions. What features do you think we should add or improve? Your ideas will help us create a better public space for everyone.
> City Council

**Task 1.1 – Informal Letter.** Write to your friend who often visits the park with you. Share your ideas about what should be changed or added. *Write about 50 words.*

**Task 1.2 – Formal Letter.** Write to the city council, giving your opinions and specific suggestions for the park renovation. *Write 120–150 words.*

**Task 2 – Forum Post.** Online discussion: *"Should governments invest more money in space exploration or solving problems on Earth?"* Give your opinion and support it with examples. *Write 180–200 words.*

## Javoblar

### Task 1.1 – Informal Letter

Hey Tom,

Did you hear the city council wants to renovate our local park? I think they should add more benches and shaded areas, especially near the playground. It'd also be great to have a small outdoor café or a fitness zone. Let's send in some suggestions together—maybe they'll listen!

See you soon,
Jake

### Task 1.2 – Formal Letter

Dear City Council,

Thank you for seeking community input on the upcoming park renovation. As a regular visitor to the park, I'm pleased to offer some suggestions.

Firstly, I believe adding more shaded seating areas would greatly benefit all visitors, especially the elderly and parents watching their children at the playground. The current seating is limited and often exposed to direct sunlight.

Secondly, installing a small outdoor fitness area with basic exercise equipment would encourage a healthier lifestyle and make the park more appealing to all age groups. A dedicated walking or jogging path would also be appreciated.

Additionally, improving lighting in the evening and adding more waste bins could enhance safety and cleanliness. A small café or refreshment kiosk could also provide a social hub for park users.

Thank you again for involving the public in this process. I look forward to seeing the improvements.

Sincerely,
Jake Williams

### Task 2 – Forum Post

I believe that while space exploration is important, governments should prioritize solving pressing problems on Earth first. The challenges we face—like poverty, climate change, healthcare, and education—require immediate attention and resources. For example, millions of people worldwide still lack access to clean water and basic healthcare, and addressing these issues can have an immediate, tangible impact on improving lives.

That being said, space exploration does bring long-term benefits, such as technological advancements and new scientific knowledge. The development of satellite technology has led to better weather forecasting and communication systems, while space research has contributed to innovations like GPS and medical imaging. However, these benefits should not come at the expense of addressing Earth's urgent issues.

In the end, governments can strike a balance by allocating funds for both—investing in space while also focusing on solving critical issues here at home. The key is to ensure that the allocation of resources is proportionate to the immediate needs of the population and the long-term benefits that space exploration can provide. Prioritizing Earth first doesn't mean abandoning space exploration, but rather finding a responsible way to do both.
